An accessible tool designed to estimate pelvic tilt alignment from side-view photographs, provide ergonomic recommendations, and connect users with qualified healthcare professionals.

Screening Aid Notice: PelviCheck is an educational and preliminary screening tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace clinical evaluations by physical therapists or physicians.

Did You Know?

Asymptomatic pelvic tilt variations are common: research indicates 85% of males and 75% of females exhibit anterior pelvic tilt naturally without symptoms.

Before Starting the Assessment

  1. Posture: Stand in your usual, comfortable standing posture.
  2. Body Position: Ensure your body is properly positioned within the camera view.
  3. Clothing: Wear clothing that allows the relevant hip and pelvic landmarks to be identified.
  4. Camera Setup: Follow the recommended camera placement and distance.
  5. Lighting and Image Quality: Use adequate lighting and ensure the side-view photo is clear and unobstructed.
  6. Privacy and Limitations: Some landmarks may not be visible. Do not rely on results if image quality or landmark visibility is insufficient. PelviCheck is not a diagnostic tool.
